# ADR-001 - Project Vision

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Organizations struggle to access data, knowledge, and decision-support information across disconnected systems and repositories.

Generative AI and Agentic AI offer new ways to bridge these gaps while introducing governance, risk, and operational challenges.

## Decision

Build an Enterprise AI & Decision Intelligence Platform rather than a traditional chatbot.

The platform must support:

- Conversational AI
- Analytics
- Database Access
- Knowledge Retrieval
- Governance
- Auditability
- Risk Controls

## Consequences

### Positive

- Aligns with enterprise needs
- Supports future expansion
- Demonstrates modern AI architecture

### Negative

- Increased implementation complexity
- Larger scope requiring incremental MVP delivery
